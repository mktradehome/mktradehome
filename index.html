My Mktradehome Journey

A personal record of the idea, the work, the lessons and the progress.

Where the idea began

Mktradehome started with a simple goal: build a personal trading assistant that could help me study the markets more carefully while I trade on Exness. I wanted useful analysis and alerts, while keeping control of every trading decision in my own hands.

Building the foundation

The project grew step by step: setting up the dashboard, connecting market prices, adding BTC/USD, EUR/USD, XAU/USD and USD/JPY, and creating the 4H, 1H, 15M and 5M analysis flow.

Learning through challenges

There were real technical challenges along the way, including connecting Supabase, building the MT5 bridge, testing Demo and Real account monitoring, and fixing the small details that make the dashboard dependable.

Making it more useful

Mktradehome now includes market analysis, account monitoring, trade safety records, Telegram alert support and an AI Support chat. Each feature is being tested carefully before relying on it.

The vision ahead

The goal is to keep improving Mktradehome into a clear, reliable personal trading companion: honest about uncertainty, careful with risk, and always leaving the final decision with me.

This journey is still being written. Every improvement, test and lesson becomes part of the story.
